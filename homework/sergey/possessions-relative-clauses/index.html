1 · Relative clauses

Choose the complete sentence

Decide whether the clause identifies the noun or adds extra information. Pay attention to commas.

1. Only one of several neighbours lent the speaker a drill.
The clause identifies which neighbour.
2. The speaker has already identified the camera as my old camera; the date is extra information.
Extra information is separated by commas.
3. The handle belonged to the suitcase.
Whose can show that a part belongs to a thing as well as to a person.
4. The clause identifies the place.
Use a relative word for a place.
5. I own three tablets. Two are fast enough for work, but the one from my sister is not.
The clause that my sister gave me identifies one tablet out of three. It is essential information, so this is a defining relative clause without commas.
6. The family has one washing machine; its repair history is additional information.
Use commas and which for additional information.
