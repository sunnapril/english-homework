Seven short conversations

Audio script — open after listening

Conversation 1
Waiter: How’s your dish, madam?
Customer: The vegetables are a bit raw, actually.
Waiter: Oh, I’m sorry to hear that. I can ask the kitchen to cook them a bit longer.
Customer: Yes, OK. If you would.
Waiter: Of course, madam. Just bear with me a few minutes …
Customer: Sure.

Conversation 2
Customer: Excuse me.
Waiter: Yes, sir.
Customer: Is this dish supposed to be so spicy?
Waiter: Well, yes, it is one of our more spicy dishes.
Customer: It’s just too spicy for me.
Waiter: Oh, I’m sorry to hear that, sir.
Customer: Could I choose something else?
Waiter: Yes, of course. If you’d like to take a look at the menu …
Customer: Yes, OK. Sorry, I just wasn’t expecting something quite so hot.
Waiter: That’s absolutely fine. I’ll mention it to the chef. Do you know what you’d like instead?
Customer: Not yet. Could you give me a couple of minutes to take a look?
Waiter: Of course. I’ll come back.

Conversation 3
Waiter: How’s your food, guys?
Customer: It’s nice, but the side dishes haven’t come yet.
Waiter: Oh, I’m really sorry about that. Something must have gone wrong. I’ll go and check where they are.
Customer: Thanks.
[The waiter checks and returns.]
Waiter: I’m really sorry, but they forgot to do them. They’ll be with you in a couple of minutes. I’ll deduct them from your bill. And can I offer you another drink, compliments of the house?
Customers: That sounds good. Great, I’ll have a fresh orange juice, please.

Conversation 4
Customer: Excuse me.
Waiter: How can I help?
Customer: Could you warm this up for me?
Waiter: Oh, I’m sorry. Is it not warm enough?
Customer: No, it’s completely cold in the middle.
Waiter: Oh, of course. I’m sorry about that. I’ll do that for you right now.
Customer: Thank you.

Conversation 5
Customer: Excuse me! This isn’t what I ordered. I ordered the vegetarian pie, not the meat one.
Waiter: Oh, that’s odd. Are you sure it’s meat?
Customer: Totally sure.
Waiter: I’ll check it out for you. Hold on a minute.
[The waiter checks and returns.]
Waiter: The chef says it is vegetarian.
Customer: Well, it’s not, because I’m looking at pieces of meat right now. Could I speak to the manager?
Waiter: I’ll see if I can find her. I won’t be a minute.

Conversation 6
Customer: Excuse me. We need to be somewhere in half an hour and the food still hasn’t come yet. Could you check on the order for me?
Waiter: Yes, of course. What did you order?
Customer: I ordered the lasagne and my friend ordered the pancakes.
Waiter: OK, let me check for you. Just give me a moment.

Conversation 7
Waiter: How is everything?
Customer: Well, the sauce is very creamy, which is nice, but I asked for no tomatoes and this dish has got tomatoes in it.
Waiter: I’m sorry about that. I’ll change it for you right away.
Customer: OK, thanks.

1 · First listening

Identify the problem

Choose the main problem in each conversation. Listen once without opening the script.

1. Conversation 1
What does raw tell you about the vegetables?
2. Conversation 2
The customer says the dish is hotter than expected.
3. Conversation 3
The waiter later offers a free drink, but that is compensation, not the original problem.
4. Conversation 4
The customer asks the waiter to warm something up.
5. Conversation 5
The customer can see an ingredient that should not be there.
6. Conversation 6
They need to leave soon.
7. Conversation 7
The customer says something positive before identifying the problem.
