1 · Reading

Was the argument worth it?

Small disagreements can become surprisingly serious. Read three people’s stories.

Story 1 · A family dinner

I usually get on well with my partner’s family, but his aunt and I do not always see eye to eye. At a family dinner, she kept insisting that everyone should eat meat. I explained that I had been a vegetarian for years, but she said my choice was childish and refused to take it seriously. I did not want to contradict an older relative, but I finally told her that I had an issue with the way she was speaking to me. The conversation became uncomfortable, and neither of us apologised.

Story 2 · The new employee

During my first month at a software company, several coworkers gave me all the repetitive jobs. At first I accepted this because I was inexperienced. However, I soon realised that I was learning almost nothing, so I said I wanted more challenging work. Two colleagues ganged up on me and claimed that I was being disrespectful. Our team leader eventually intervened. She agreed that the work should be shared more fairly, and my coworkers finally backed down.

Story 3 · The shared kitchen

My flatmate picks a fight with me whenever I cook. She wants every pan and plate washed immediately, while I prefer to eat first and clear up afterwards. I admit that I sometimes leave things for too long, but she also tends to exaggerate the problem. We have tried to find a compromise, but the underlying issue is that we have very different ideas about what “tidy” means.
