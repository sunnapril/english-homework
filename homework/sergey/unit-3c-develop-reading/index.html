1 · Dictionary strategy

Choose the useful entry

A monolingual dictionary often gives several meanings. Choose the definition that fits the sentence.

1. She tried to impose her opinion on the whole team.
The object is an opinion, and the phrase includes on someone.
2. Neither side was willing to back down.
The context is an argument, not physical movement.
3. It is a sensitive issue.
Issue refers to a topic.
2 · Magazine article

The hidden cost of being right

At first, disagreement may seem useful: it exposes weak ideas and prevents a group from accepting the first solution it hears. Yet an argument becomes less productive when winning matters more than solving the problem. In one software team, two developers disagreed about a feature. One wanted a fast release; the other insisted on further testing. Both had evidence, but each began to treat the discussion as a personal contest.

The team leader did not immediately choose either side. Instead, she asked each developer to describe the other person’s strongest argument. This changed the tone. They realised that the underlying issue was not competence but risk: one feared missing the deadline, while the other feared causing inconvenience to customers. The leader let them design a limited release with extra monitoring. Neither person got everything they wanted, but the compromise protected the deadline and the users.

The lesson is not that people should always back down. A valuable member of a team sometimes has to contradict others or defend an unpopular view. However, intense rivalry can make people ignore information that threatens their position. Productive disagreement requires a direct answer to the real question, respectful language and a willingness to revise a plan.

Main idea

Look at both the opening and the final paragraph.
3 · Read for detail
5. Why did the leader ask them to state the other person’s strongest argument?
The next sentence explains the effect.
6. What compromise did they find?
Find the final sentence of paragraph 2.
7. According to the writer, contradicting others is…
Read the second sentence of the final paragraph.
4 · Accuracy
8. The leader did not immediately choose ___ .
Use either + singular noun.
9. The leader ___ a limited release. (let / they / design)
Object pronoun + base verb, no to.
10. The new process produced ___ complaints.
Complaints are countable plural.
11. The compromise caused ___ inconvenience.
Inconvenience is uncountable here.
