Script · open after listening
Jake: Right. I’m taking a photo of us before we go to the school costume party. Olly: Let’s see it! Wow! We look so good in these costumes. Um, what are you doing, Jake? Jake: I’m sharing it on my social media page right now. Olly: Jake, stop! I don’t want you to share that photo online. Jake: Why not, Olly? Olly: Lots of reasons. Firstly, because we’re wearing these cat costumes! Jake: So? I think we look really funny! Olly: Me too, but think about the future. Do you want people to find this photo of you in five years’ time? For example, a university or a business? Jake: And see me in this costume? Um, no, not really. But only my friends and family can see my photos. Olly: What if one of your friends downloads our photo and posts it on their social media? Jake: Oh, I guess you’re right. Olly: And another thing. Now everybody knows that we’re going out this evening, and we aren’t at home! Jake: Why is that a problem? Olly: Think about it. In this photo, we’re standing here in your bedroom, and everybody can see all your expensive things, like your volleyball medals, for example. Jake: Ah … you’re right! I’m deleting it now! Olly: Thanks, Jake.
