3 · Use the vocabulary

What does the behaviour tell you?

Choose the most suitable adjective and explain your choice in one or two sentences. There may be more than one defensible answer, so give evidence.

1. A project leader continues with the original plan even after the team presents strong evidence that it will fail. Is the leader determined or stubborn? Why?
Useful language: refuse to change your mind · take evidence into account
2. A new employee volunteers to present a risky idea to the directors, but listens carefully to their concerns. Is the employee bold, confident or arrogant? Why?
Useful language: be willing to take a risk · listen to feedback
3. A colleague notices that a visitor is nervous, starts a friendly conversation and puts them at ease. Which adjective fits the colleague best? Explain.
Recycle: put someone at ease
4. An inspector has to enforce an unpopular law. They apply the same rules to everyone but listen to each person’s circumstances. Which two adjectives could describe the inspector? Explain.
Recycle: enforce a law · take circumstances into account
