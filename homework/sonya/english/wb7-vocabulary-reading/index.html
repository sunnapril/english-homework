Object pronouns

Choose the correct alternative. These are the six original Workbook sentences.

Quick map: I → me · you → you · he → him · she → her · it → it · we → us · they → them
16. The teacher is here. Listen to them / him!
One male teacher.
17. I’m in an exam. Don’t talk to it / me!
The speaker says “I’m in an exam”. Which object pronoun refers to the speaker?
