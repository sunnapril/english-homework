Guard: Stop! Take off your sunglasses, please. Is this your yellow bag?
Boy: Yes, it is.
Guard: Open it, please. What's in this purple box? Is it a mobile phone?
Boy: No, that's an old video game.
Guard: Are these your keys?
Boy: Yes, they are.
Guard: And what are these?
Boy: Those are my headphones and that's my new mobile phone.
Guard: Take out your mobile phone, please. And these orange books?
Boy: Those are my diaries. This is slow. Can I go?
Guard: Not so fast! What's this blue thing?
