02 · Forms

Complete the sentence

1. Yesterday we (decide) to make a dessert.
2. We (not buy) it at the shop.
3. What (you / try) last weekend?
4. The final result (be) excellent.
Action: past form in a statement; did/didn’t + base verb in questions and negatives. Be: was/were.
03 · Word order

Build two sentences

1. yesterday / after dancing / thirsty / she / felt / .
2. last night / decide / what / they / did / to make / ?
Question word → did → subject → base verb → rest of the sentence.
