8Task 4 · Listening · SB Track 2.01

Lynn’s interview day

Show script
Tony: So, how did your job interview go the other day, Lynn? Lynn: Oh, it was terrible! Tony: Oh, no! What happened? Lynn: Well, it was just one of those days, you know? It started badly and just got worse. First of all, my alarm didn’t go off. When I woke up, my clock was on the floor. I think the cat played with it during the night and broke it. Tony: Oh, no! Lynn: Oh, yes! I got up late and didn’t have time for breakfast. So, I started the day angry and hungry! Tony: Oh dear. Lynn: Then I got into my car and – I couldn’t believe it – the battery was dead! So, I had to take the bus. Well, you can imagine that at this point, I was really stressed. Tony: Let me guess – you missed the bus? Lynn: Ha, no. I caught it, but half way there, it broke down! Tony: So, what did you do? Lynn: Well, we had to wait for the next bus, but then it started raining really heavily and, of course, I didn’t have an umbrella. When I arrived for the interview, I was soaking wet. Tony: Oh, Lynn. Lynn: It got worse, though. I had to give a presentation as part of the interview. I felt quite relaxed about it, actually. But just before I started, I spilt a glass of water all over my laptop. I tried to turn it on, but it didn’t work. Tony: Oh, no! Lynn: Yeah, so by now I was really nervous, and my presentation was terrible. I even forgot to say some important things … the interviewers weren’t happy. Tony: Oh, I’m sorry to hear that, Lynn. Lynn: I know, thanks, Tony. I was so happy when that day ended! I’ve got another interview next week, though. I’ll just make sure the cat sleeps in another room! Tony: Ha, good idea!
