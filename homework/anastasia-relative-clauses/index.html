Before you start

People, things and places

who / that
a person who helps me
a person that helps me
which / that
a book which explains it
a book that explains it
where
a place where people meet
Can we use that for people? Yes. In a defining relative clause, both are possible: the woman who called and the woman that called. Who is usually more natural for people, especially in writing. Do not use that after a comma: My aunt, who lives in Riga, …
