4 · How much do we need?

Countable plural
a few tomatoes · some leaves · not many anchovies
Uncountable
a little mayo · some sour cream · not much salt

In questions: use any when you do not know the answer. No means zero: There is no mayo.

Addsour cream; two spoonfuls are enough.

Do we havemayo in the fridge?

1. Can you count separate items? Use a few.
2. Is it a substance or amount? Use a little.
3. Is it a neutral question? Usually use any.

5 · Explain one recipe step clearly

This is not another full dish description. Give precise instructions for one stage only.

Choose one stage
prepare the vegetables
make a simple sauce
finish and serve the dish
Useful sequence
First, …
Then, add …
Finally, serve it with …
TASK: Write three instructions. Use one quantity phrase and either slice or wedge.
First, cut the cucumber into thin slices. Then, add a little sour cream to the remaining ingredients. Finally, serve the dish with a lemon wedge.
Teacher follow-up
  1. Could you use less of one ingredient?
  2. What could you add for more flavour?
  3. Is this step quick, or does it take a long time?
