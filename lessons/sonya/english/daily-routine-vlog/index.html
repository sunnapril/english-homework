My best friend finishes school, goes home, listens to music or goes out, but I can’t see him because we make videos every day. Hanna: That’s true. Our weekends are busy too. Ex.4 · Listen again. Mark 1–8 T or F.
1. Sam buys clothes with money from his vlog.
Listen for what Sam does with the money from his vlog.
T
2. Sam starts school at 11 a.m.
Listen for the two times: 11 a.m. and half past eight.
F — Sam has lessons at half past eight.
3. Lukas and Hanna have a daily vlog.
Listen for the word that tells you how often they vlog.
T
4. Lukas and Hanna have free weekends.
What do they do on Saturday and Sunday?
F — On Saturdays they edit videos, and on Sundays they do their homework.
5. Two million people watch Demi's videos.
Listen for a number in Demi's part.
T
6. Demi can relax when she goes out.
What happens when she takes the bus or has dinner in town?
F — She can't relax because everybody takes photos of her.
7. Tom plays the guitar in a vlog.
Who actually plays the guitar: Tom or Megan?
F — Megan plays the guitar in her vlog.
8. Megan doesn't like vlogging.
Listen for Megan's feeling about vlogging before the problem with comments.
F — Megan loves vlogging.
