My Unit 1 answer

Easy

One useful change

Describe one small change you would like to make.

Path: I’d like to … → I usually … → This month I’m … → It could be useful because …

I’d like to go for a walk more often. I usually stay at home in the evening, but this month I’m walking after dinner twice a week. It could be useful because I spend less time online.
Challenge

Advice for a change

Imagine a friend wants to develop a new habit.

Path: My advice is … → First … → Then … → When … → Don’t forget to … in advance.

My advice is to choose one clear goal. First, make a simple plan. Then take one small step. When the plan is difficult, ask for support. Don’t forget to prepare in advance.
