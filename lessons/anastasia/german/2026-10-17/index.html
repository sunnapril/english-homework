Zahlen korrigieren

Die Wörter sind falsch. Schreibe die richtige Form.

21 = einsundzwanzig
For 21, put the ones before undzwanzig, but do not add -s to ein.
einundzwanzig
17 = siebenzehn
The number 17 has a shortened form of sieben.
siebzehn
45 = vierundfünfzig
The written number must match 45, not 54.
fünfundvierzig
16 = sechszehn
The word sechs loses the final -s here.
sechzehn
30 = dreizehn
This is a multiple of ten, not a teen number.
dreißig
71 = siebzigundeins
Start with the ones, then und, then the tens.
einundsiebzig
