8Meaning contrast

Skill or ability?

ability
a general capacity: what a person is capable of doing
the ability to learn quickly
Humans have the ability to use language.
skill
a specific thing you learn and improve through practice
legal skills · riding skills
Shooting accurately is a useful skill.
They sometimes overlap. If you mean a particular trained technique, choose skill. If you mean a person’s broader capacity, especially in the pattern the ability to do something, choose ability.
Writing clear legal documents is an important professional ___.
Young children gradually develop the ___ to understand other people’s feelings.
Listening and negotiation are useful communication ___.
After the accident, he temporarily lost the ___ to move his left hand.
«Гуманитарий»: in everyday English, say I’m more of an arts person or I’m a humanities person. If education matters: I studied the humanities or I have a humanities background.
8One instead of repetition

One or ones?

Use one/ones only when we mean another or a particular item of the same kind.
one replaces one singular countable noun: Which target? — The red one. (= the red target)
ones replaces a plural countable noun: Which exercises? — The last two ones. (= the last two exercises)

We often use them after which, this, that, another, the other or an adjective. We do not normally use them instead of uncountable nouns.
I need a new laptop. My old ___ is too slow.
Which exercises were difficult? The last two ___.
I don’t like this target. Can I use the other ___?

8So and too

Small words, four jobs

so + adjective = very / to a great degree
The lesson was so interesting!

clause, so + result = therefore / because of that
It was late, so I went home.
too at the end = also
I enjoyed it too.

too + adjective = excessively: the degree creates a problem or makes something impossible
The target was too far away to see clearly.
It was farther than I needed or could manage.
Compare: The suitcase was so heavy! = very heavy. · The suitcase was too heavy for me to lift = I could not lift it.
The box is 60 cm wide, but the shelf is only 40 cm. The box is ___ wide to fit on the shelf.
I was tired, ___ I went home early.
The view was ___ beautiful!
My friend wants to try horse riding ___.
