1 · Maybe, perhaps or probably?

maybe / perhaps

Both mean “possibly”. Perhaps sounds a little more formal.

возможно; может быть
probably

Use it when you think something is more likely than not.

вероятно; скорее всего
1. The office lights are on, so Elena is still at work.
2. I’m not sure. the document is in my other bag.
3. She prepared carefully, so she will give a confident answer.
4. we should check the address before we leave.
Speak: What will you probably do this week? What might you do?
