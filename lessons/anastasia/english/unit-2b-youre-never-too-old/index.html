12SB p.16 · Reading

You don’t have to be young to do amazing things

1 · Kimani Maruge

Kimani Maruge was born in Kenya. When he was a child, families had to pay for school, so he didn’t learn to read and write. In 2003, primary schools became free. He started school for the first time at 84. Learning wasn’t easy, but he worked hard. In 2005, he travelled to New York to speak at the United Nations about free education.

2 · Harriette Thompson

Harriette was born in 1923 in the US. She first ran the San Diego marathon at 76. She ran it almost every year until 2015. When she was 94, she became the oldest woman to complete a marathon. She finished in 7 hours, 24 minutes and collected more than $100,000 for charity.

3 · Laila Denmark

Laila was born in 1898 in Atlanta. She wanted to become a doctor and help children. Studying medicine wasn’t usual for women then, and she was the only woman in a class of 52 students. She didn’t retire until she was 103 and lived for another eleven years.
