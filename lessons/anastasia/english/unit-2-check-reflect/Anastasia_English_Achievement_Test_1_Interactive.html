Why do some sports stars do better than others? They all show great skill in their sport, of course. 1 h

But some of the very best athletes think a lot about how to be successful. Take Wayne Gretzky, for example. He is probably the best ice hockey player ever. He says that if you don’t try, you will never be successful.

Venus Williams and Serena Williams are two of the best tennis players in history. Serena says it is OK if other people doubt you, if you believe you can succeed. Venus agrees.

Many people choose Pelé, Ronaldo, Maradona or Messi. Messi believes that doing what you love is important.

Lydia Ko is a golfer from New Zealand. She sets small goals which lead to success.

Lewis Hamilton says that when he doesn’t win, he learns lessons that make him stronger. He believes we can learn something new every day.
