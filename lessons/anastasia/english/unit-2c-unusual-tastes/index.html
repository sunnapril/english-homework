2 · Choose the natural structure

First choose the complete natural question. Then use the pattern yourself.

1. They were in a hurry. Ask for the reason.

2. She checked something carefully. Ask about the object.

3. Ask about the number of passengers.

4. Ask about the arrival time.

5. You want to know which person did the action. Choose the correct question.

Question word + auxiliary + subject + main verb: When did the train arrive?
Be changes position: Why were they in a hurry?

Compare who:
Who looked after the plants? — who did the action, so there is no did.
Who did you look after? — you did the action; who is the person receiving it, so we use did.
WITH YOUR TEACHER: Choose a different topic — last weekend, work or a journey. Ask three true questions: one with did, one with was/were, and one beginning with Who.
