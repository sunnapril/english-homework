1 · The rest or remaining?

the rest of + noun

Add the sour cream to the rest of the ingredients.

Добавьте сметану к остальным ингредиентам.
remaining + noun

Add the sour cream to the remaining ingredients.

Добавьте сметану к оставшимся ингредиентам.

Both are correct. But not rest ingredients or remaining of the ingredients.

Add the onions to the vegetables.
Serve the soup with the herbs.

3 · Countable, uncountable, quantity

Countable

a / an dish · a few anchovies · some herbs

Uncountable

a little / a bit of mayo · some sour cream

some: positive sentences · any: questions and negatives · no: zero.

a lot of / lots of: large quantity · a few / a little: small quantity.

a few — с тем, что можно посчитать; a little — с веществом или общей массой. There are no anchovies = анчоусов нет.
There are anchovies in this vegetarian version.
Is there sour cream?
Add fresh herbs.
