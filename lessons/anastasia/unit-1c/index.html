10Reading · Roadmap p.10

A simple month

Marek and Kim usually live in a big city, but this month they are trying a very different lifestyle. They are staying in a small house in a forest. The house has no electricity.

Every day, they get up at 5.30 and have a simple breakfast. Then they pick vegetables in the garden and collect wood. They normally finish at about six. In the evening, they play cards, read or sit outside, and they go to bed at around nine.

Today Marek is using a computer in a shop to write this update because the couple come into town only once a week. Kim is looking for warm blankets. Marek says they aren’t missing television and are enjoying the experience so far.

Present simple or present continuous?

Present simple
routines and facts
They get up early every day.
Present continuous
now or a temporary situation
They are trying a new lifestyle this month.

4

5

6

Question: What is one temporary change in your life these days?

Start: These days, I’m … . I usually …, but at the moment …

These days, I’m learning to cook more dishes. I usually make very simple meals, but at the moment I’m trying new recipes.

11Speaking goal

Try a new lifestyle for one month

Choose one realistic change. Make short notes, then describe your normal life and the temporary change.

1 · Change

What are you trying?

I’m trying a no-phone evening twice a week for one month.
2 · Normal routine

What do you usually do?

I usually check my messages several times in the evening.
3 · Now

What are you doing differently?

At the moment, I’m reading or going for a walk instead.
4 · Result

How is it going?

So far, it is difficult, but I have plenty of time for useful things.
Your answer path:
I’m trying … for one month. → I usually … → But at the moment, I’m … → So far, … → I think this change will / won’t become a habit because …
