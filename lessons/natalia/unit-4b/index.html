8Reading · Roadmap p.32 Ex.2

More than a name

A successful brand is more than a recognisable name. First, it needs a clear identity: colours, a logo or a phrase that people remember immediately.

It also communicates a personality. A company may appear practical and reliable, young and adventurous, or traditional and elegant. Customers often choose brands whose personality feels right for them.

Finally, a strong brand shows its values through its actions. It may reduce waste, offer excellent customer service or make products that last. A company’s behaviour needs to support the message it gives customers.
