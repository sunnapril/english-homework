5. Defining relative clauses

The relative word tells us what kind of information follows.

people
who / that
things
which / that
places
where
possession
whose
That can refer to both people and things: an actor that I admire; a film that I recommend.
Speaking · PersonalComplete two true sentences: “A person who…” and “A place where…”

7. Keep it or omit it?

We can omit who/which/that only when another subject follows it.

Keep: It’s a film that makes me laugh. (that = subject)
Omit: It’s a film (that) I watch every year. (I = subject)
