1. Quick choice — then speak

Choose the word that fits. Open the meaning only if you need it.

compete
try to be more successful than another person or group
competition
a situation in which people or companies try to win or be more successful
competence
the ability to do something well

Two cafés have opened on the same street. They ______ for the same customers.

There is a lot of ______ between them.

Speak: Both cafés are trying to attract the same customers. Do customers usually benefit from this? How?
Think about prices, service, choice or quality. A short answer is enough.

2. Same idea, different shape

persuaded
Speak: Marta changed her mind. What can make you change your mind about a trip?
Weather, price, who is going, comfort, a special event…

3. Suggest — choose what you want to say

Use the pattern that fits your meaning. Open the explanation only if you need it.

suggest doing
recommend an activity: I suggest checking the details again.
suggest that + person + base verb
recommend that somebody do something: She suggested that Mark review the figures.
suggest something to somebody
recommend a thing or idea to a person: He suggested another restaurant to us.

The figures need another review. She suggested that Mark ______ them before lunch.

After suggest that + person, use the base verb here.

Your friend has one free afternoon in St Petersburg but no plan.

Speak: What would you suggest?
You can start with I suggest… or I suggest that…

Your family is choosing where to have dinner. One place looks good, but it is usually crowded.

Speak: What would you suggest?
You can suggest a place, an action or a different time.
