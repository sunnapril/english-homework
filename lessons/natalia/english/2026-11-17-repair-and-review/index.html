Find the mistake

Each sentence has one mistake. Tap the part with the mistake, then write the correct version of that part.

1We spent three days in the town Suzdal and visited five old churches.
the town ___ Suzdal: one small word is missing.
We spent three days in the town of Suzdal and visited five old churches.
2My washing machine broke, but I found a good master who fixed it in an hour.
“Мастер” in English is not “master”. Who repairs things?
My washing machine broke, but I found a good repairman who fixed it in an hour.
3The shop doesn’t sell repair parts for this old printer.
Extra parts you keep or buy to replace broken ones.
The shop doesn’t sell spare parts for this old printer.
4When I first saw the painting, I thought it isn’t finished, but it was.
thought is in the past, so the next verb goes to the past too.
When I first saw the painting, I thought it wasn’t finished but it was.
5In general, it wouldn’t be cheaper to repair an old kettle.
A general fact, not an imaginary situation.
In general, it isn’t cheaper to repair an old kettle.
6The guide gave us an advice about the best time to visit the museum.
advice is uncountable.
The guide gave us some advice about the best time to visit the museum.
7I want you check the bill again, please.
want + person + ? + verb
I want you to check the bill again, please.
8My friend suggested to visit the gallery in the morning.
suggest + ?
My friend suggested visiting the gallery in the morning.
