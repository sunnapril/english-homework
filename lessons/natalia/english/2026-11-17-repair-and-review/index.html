Find the mistake

Each sentence has one mistake. Tap the part with the mistake, then write the correct version of that part.

1We spent three days in the town Suzdal and visited five old churches.
the town ___ Suzdal: one small word is missing.
We spent three days in the town of Suzdal and visited five old churches.
2My washing machine broke, but I found a good master who fixed it in an hour.
“Мастер” in English is not “master”. Who repairs things?
My washing machine broke, but I found a good repairman who fixed it in an hour.
3The shop doesn’t sell repair parts for this old printer.
Extra parts you keep or buy to replace broken ones.
The shop doesn’t sell spare parts for this old printer.
4When I first saw the painting, I thought it isn’t finished, but it was.
thought is in the past, so the next verb goes to the past too.
When I first saw the painting, I thought it wasn’t finished but it was.
5In general, it wouldn’t be cheaper to repair an old kettle.
A general fact, not an imaginary situation.
In general, it isn’t cheaper to repair an old kettle.
6The dessert consisted from three layers: chocolate, cream and raspberries.
consist + which preposition?
The dessert consisted of three layers: chocolate, cream and raspberries.
7On Sunday I cooked a cake with raspberries for my mother’s birthday.
We don’t usually say cook with a cake.
On Sunday I baked a cake with raspberries for my mother’s birthday.
8After twenty years, he seemed a different kind person to me.
kind ___ person: one small word is missing.
After twenty years, he seemed a different kind of person to me.

Unexpected results

Things don’t always go as planned. These phrases help you tell what happened in the end.

turn out (to be) + adjective / noun — the result was different from what you expected: The lecture looked boring, but it turned out to be really interesting.
end up + -ing / + place — you finally do something you didn’t plan: Nobody wanted to organise the party, so I ended up doing everything myself.
in the end = finally, after some time · at the end (of something) = at the last part: at the end of the film
better / worse than I expected · not as good as the first one · by mistake · luckily / unfortunately
