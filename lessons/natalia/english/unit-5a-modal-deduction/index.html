2 · Natural English

Correct one word or phrase

1. I was excited from the exhibition.

HintChange the preposition after excited.
Correct answerI was excited about the exhibition.

2. Her style is loose.

HintLoose describes an item of clothing, not a whole style.
Correct answerShe has a relaxed style.

3. Her impression about modern clothes was strange.

HintUse view of and a more precise adjective.
Correct answerHer view of modern clothes seemed unusual.

4. She is very fashionable: her clothes are always attractive and well chosen.

HintThe intended meaning is “attractive and well chosen”, not “currently popular”.
Correct answerShe is very stylish.

6 · Grammar Bank p.125 · Ex. 2

Rewrite using a modal verb

Keep the meaning of the original sentence.

1. Perhaps we will be a few minutes late.
HintStart with We might…
Correct answerWe might be a few minutes late.
2. I’m not sure, but perhaps this is David’s house.
HintUse a possibility modal + be.
Possible answerThis might / may / could be David’s house.
3. I’m sure the café is near here.
HintUse the modal for a strong positive deduction.
Correct answerThe café must be near here.
4. Perhaps Diana won’t come to the meeting.
HintPut not after the modal.
Possible answerDiana might / may not come to the meeting.
5. Take an umbrella. Perhaps it will rain later.
HintUse a possibility modal + the base form rain.
Possible answerIt might / may / could rain later.
6. I’m sure this isn’t her coat. It’s too small.
HintUse the modal for an almost impossible deduction.
Correct answerThis can’t be her coat.
7. The bill is £25. I’m sure there is a mistake.
HintUse a strong positive deduction.
Correct answerThere must be a mistake.
