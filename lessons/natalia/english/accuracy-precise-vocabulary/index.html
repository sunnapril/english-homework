2 · Clothes

Dress up, wear or have on?

wear is the general verb: She wears glasses (usual) / She is wearing a jacket (now). have something on describes only the current state: She has a jacket on. We do not normally say She is having a jacket on. dress up means choosing special or formal clothes for an occasion.
dress up

make yourself look more formal or special for an occasion

wear / be wearing

general habit or an action happening now

have ... on

the clothes currently on somebody; a state, not a continuous action

be dressed in

3B · Location

Three natural ways to locate a place

Our house is in ...

the most natural everyday choice

Our house is located in ...

neutral, useful when exact location matters

Our house is situated in ...

correct but more formal; common in written descriptions

1. Correct the missing verb: Our house situated in a quiet neighbourhood.
HintSituated needs the verb be.
Our house is situated in a quiet neighbourhood.
2. You are casually telling a friend where you live. Choose the most natural version.
HintEveryday conversation usually uses the shortest version.
Our house is in a quiet neighbourhood.
3. An office website gives its exact position on a map. Complete the sentence:
Our office near the central station.
HintThe neutral expression emphasises an exact position.
Our office is located near the central station.
4. A formal brochure describes a hotel and its surroundings. Complete:
The hotel in a peaceful suburb.
HintA polished written description often uses the more formal expression.
The hotel is situated in a peaceful suburb.

6 · Small 5B bridge

Which office would work better?

A small accounting team is choosing between two offices. Compare the practical consequences, then recommend one.

Office A · city centre

high rent · beside a major landmark · little storage space · easy for clients to reach

Office B · quiet suburb

low rent · large storage room · 35 minutes from the centre · free parking

1. Which office would be cheaper to run, and why?
2. Which office would be more convenient for clients?

7 · Controlled deduction

What must, might or can't be true?

Use only the evidence given. No invented story is necessary.

1. Every window is dark, the gate is locked and there are no cars outside.
Nobody can be at home. / The owners must be away.
2. The rent is very low, but the flat is beside a busy railway line.
The low rent might be connected to the noise.
3. There are removal boxes in every room and no pictures on the walls.
They must be moving in or moving out.
