Question tags

2. Turn the grammar around

A question tag turns a statement into a short check. First find whether the statement is positive or negative. Then use the opposite form in the tag.

positive statement

Japan was memorable, wasn’t it?

+ → −
negative statement

You don’t enjoy running, do you?

− → +
Three decisions

1. Find the verb or auxiliary. 2. Reverse positive ↔ negative. 3. Replace the subject with a pronoun.

She can help, can’t she? · The meetings haven’t started, have they?

Choose the engine

3. Which auxiliary comes back?

If the statement already contains be, have or a modal, repeat it in the tag. With an ordinary Present or Past Simple verb, bring back do / does / did.

be

It is useful, isn’t it?

Present Simple

They work here, don’t they?

Past Simple

He called, didn’t he?

modal / perfect

You can, can’t you?
She has left, hasn’t she?

One useful special case

Let’s do it together, shall we?

Voice changes meaning

4. Agreement or a real check?

The grammar stays the same, but intonation shows what the speaker wants.

Falling ↘

You are almost certain and invite agreement.

That was a useful shortcut, wasn’t it? ↘

Rising ↗

You are genuinely uncertain and want confirmation.

The train leaves at 18:40, doesn’t it? ↗

Vocabulary Bank 6C

7. Environmental issues — precise choices

Try to recall or infer each word first. Open the English meaning only when you need it; Russian is a separate optional step.

reduce · reuse · recycle
reduce — make an amount smaller
reuse — use the same object again
recycle — process waste into usable material
reduce — сокращать; reuse — использовать снова; recycle — перерабатывать
protect · support · damage · injure
protect — keep safe
support — help a person or organisation
damage — harm a thing or system
injure — hurt a person or animal
protect — защищать; support — поддерживать; damage — наносить вред предмету или системе; injure — ранить человека или животное
throw away · waste · destroy
throw away — discard something
waste — use something carelessly or unnecessarily
destroy — damage something completely
throw away — выбрасывать; waste — тратить впустую; destroy — уничтожать
global warming · endangered animals · air pollution
global warming — the rise in the Earth’s average temperature
endangered animals — animals at risk of disappearing
air pollution — harmful substances in the air
global warming — глобальное потепление; endangered animals — животные под угрозой исчезновения; air pollution — загрязнение воздуха
