Grammar system

2. How strong is the advice?

neutral

You should check the route.
If I were you, I’d call first.

an option

You could take a taxi.
Why don’t you leave earlier?

strong / urgent

You’d better book today.
Whatever you do, don’t share the code.

worth considering

It’s worth checking.
Have you thought about taking the train?

should — нейтральный совет; could — один из вариантов; had better — настойчивый совет, часто с возможным плохим последствием; it’s worth / have you thought about — мягко предлагает рассмотреть идею.
Form matters

3. Complete both sides of the pattern

The missing part moves: sometimes it is before the situation, sometimes inside it, sometimes in the advice.

1. Have you a later train?
After about, use an -ing form.
Have you thought about taking a later train?
2. If I you, I ask for written confirmation.
The fixed advice pattern starts If I were you…
If I were you, I would ask for written confirmation.
3. You’d until the last minute.
Had better is followed by a base verb without to.
You’d better not wait until the last minute.
4. It’s the prices first.
Worth is followed by an -ing form.
It’s worth comparing the prices first.
Roadmap 6B · adapted

4. One problem, two useful options

Choose advice that is both grammatically correct and appropriate in strength.

Natural English

5. Three natural ways to locate a place

be in / near

The most natural everyday choice.

be located in / near

Neutral; useful when the exact location matters.

be situated in / near

Correct but more formal; common in written descriptions.

be in / near — обычный разговорный вариант; be located — нейтрально, когда важно точное расположение; be situated — более формально и чаще в письменных описаниях. В формах located / situated нужен глагол be.
Small corrections

6. Say exactly what you mean

remember someone

The person comes back into your mind.

remind someone

Make the person remember something.

work hard

Make a lot of effort.

hardly work

Work very little.

digital detox

A period without digital devices or social media.

downshifting

Choosing a simpler life, often with less work or income.

first-world problem

A minor inconvenience in a comfortable life.

remember — помнить / вспомнить; remind — напомнить; work hard — усердно работать; hardly work — почти не работать; digital detox — сознательный перерыв от цифровых устройств; downshifting — переход к более простой и менее напряжённой жизни; first-world problem — мелкая проблема человека, живущего в комфорте.

7. Your usual court is unavailable

Your usual badminton court will be closed for a month. What would you do, and what advice would you give your playing partner?

try another sports centre · play at a different time · book several sessions in advance

Real conversation 2

8. Get fitter without running

A friend wants to get fitter but dislikes running and most other cardio activities. What practical alternatives would you suggest?

play a racket sport · swim at a comfortable pace · take longer walks · try short strength sessions
You could start with an activity you already enjoy · It’s worth … · Whatever you do, don’t choose something you hate

9. Make a first trip to Japan memorable

A friend is planning their first trip to Japan. They want it to be memorable without filling every day with activities. What advice would you give from your own experience?

choose fewer places · leave time to explore · learn a few useful phrases · include one personal interest
