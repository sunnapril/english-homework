7 · Mixed relative clauses

Use who, which/that, whose, where, when. If the relative word can be omitted, type a hyphen: -.

1 The director ___ films often win awards started in advertising.
2 The film ___ we discussed last week is now online.
3 The actor ___ plays the detective is excellent.
4 The town ___ the story is set looks familiar.
5 The scene ___ changed my opinion comes near the end.
6 1999 was the year ___ the first film appeared.
1 whose · 2 which/that/- · 3 who/that · 4 where · 5 which/that · 6 when.
Rule: omission is possible only in 2 because we is already the subject. In 3 and 5 the relative word is the subject, so it must stay.
Speaking · Opinion
