3 · Grammar · SB p.57 + GB p.128

Two past moments

Which action happened first?

Earlier
Egor had tried several sports.
→
Later
He started playing badminton.

Full sentence: Egor had tried several sports before he started playing badminton.

Which form marks the earlier action?

Past Perfect = had + past participle. Use it for the action or situation that happened first. Use Past Simple for the action that happened second.

Negative: hadn’t + past participle · Question: Had + subject + past participle?

Useful sequence markers:
whenbeforeafterby the timeby this timealreadyjustalwayspreviouslynever

Important: with before and after, the order is sometimes already clear, so native speakers may use Past Simple for both actions. Past Perfect adds emphasis or prevents ambiguity.

ambiguity — a situation in which something has more than one possible meaning and is not completely clear. Past Perfect can remove ambiguity by showing which action happened first.
ambiguity — неоднозначность, возможность понять что-либо по-разному.

6 · Real stories

Make the grammar personal

Answer three concrete questions. Open support only if you need it. A writing box is available, but speaking is the main task.

1. Before you went to Japan, had you ever travelled that far from Moscow?

Before I went to Japan, I had / hadn’t…
The furthest I had travelled was…
What surprised me most was…
abroad · at different times in my life · source of information · gain confidence

2. Have you ever arrived somewhere and realised you had left something important at home? What happened next?

When I arrived…, I realised I had left…
By that time, …
In the end, I was able to…
urgent · last minute · postpone · get cold feet

3. Before you enrolled at university, what had you expected student life to be like? What turned out differently?

Before I enrolled, I had expected…
I hadn’t realised that…
What surprised me was…

7 · Everyday choices

Talk about real life

Use the grammar when it helps; the goal is a natural answer, not a forced Past Perfect sentence.

1. Have you ever bought something after you had compared several options? Was the research worth it?

Before I chose…, I had compared…
In the end, I went for… because…
consider · evidence · reliable source · limited choice

2. Have you ever postponed a task and then regretted it? What had happened by the time you finally started?

I had kept postponing…
By the time I started, … had already…
delay vs postpone · urgent · submit · short notice

3. Which would you rather give up for a year: travelling abroad, playing sport or watching entertainment online? Why?

I’d find it easiest to give up…
I’m much keener on… than…
The hardest one to lose would be…
rather · keen on · abroad · entertainment
