Meaning matters

4. Which word actually fits?

Read every English explanation first. Then choose the natural word in context.

delay

Make something happen later than expected, often because of a problem.

postpone

Deliberately move a planned event to a later time or date.

long

Great distance or duration.

tall

Great vertical height, especially for people and buildings.

certain

Sure that a fact is true.

confident

Sure of yourself or your ability.

attend

Go to an event.

arrange

Organise the details of something.

delay — задержать; postpone — перенести на более позднее время; long — длинный / долгий; tall — высокий; certain — уверенный в факте; confident — уверенный в себе; attend — присутствовать; arrange — организовать.
a separate kitchen

A kitchen in its own room. An open-plan kitchen / kitchen area is part of a larger living space. A detached kitchen normally means a separate building.

separate kitchen — отдельная кухня; open-plan kitchen / kitchen area — кухня, совмещённая с жилой зоной; detached kitchen — кухня в отдельном строении.

5. Make the adjective more exact

bitter

Having a sharp taste that is not sweet.

savoury

Salty or spicy rather than sweet.

bitter — горький; savoury — несладкий, солёный или пряный.

A qualifier changes the strength or limits the meaning of another word.

gradable adjective

a bit / quite / really bitter

extreme adjective

absolutely certain

qualifier — слово-уточнитель, которое усиливает, ослабляет или ограничивает значение другого слова.
