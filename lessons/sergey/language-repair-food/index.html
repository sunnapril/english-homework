Block 2 · productive use

Describe two dishes

Flavour: bland, rich, spicy, sweet, bitter · Texture: creamy, crispy, soggy, tough, tender · Effect: light, filling, greasy
Rich usually means a strong, full and pleasant flavour, but it can also describe a heavy, smooth texture: a rich chocolate flavour; a rich, thick sauce. Soggy means wet and unpleasantly soft because something has absorbed too much liquid.

Choose two dishes you have actually eaten. For each dish, explain its flavour, texture and whether it was light or filling. Give enough context for the listener to imagine the food.

Show a possible answer after discussion

The mushroom soup had a rich, creamy texture and a mild flavour. It looked like a light starter, but it was surprisingly filling. The roast potatoes were crispy outside and soft in the middle, although they became slightly soggy after they had been covered.
