1 · Retrieval diagnosis

One modifier, one job

Choose the only natural completion.

1. Katakana looks ___ angular than hiragana.
2. Of the three scripts, kanji is ___ the most demanding for many learners.
3. With practice, he can recognise the symbols ___.
4. He now reads short dialogues ___.
Keep the jobs separate: far/much + comparative (far harder, much more complex); by far + superlative (by far the hardest). With an adverb, use much/far more quickly.
Current experience: Which Japanese script is the easiest to recognise, and which is by far the hardest to remember? Explain one visual difference.
2 · Meaning and form

How strong is the claim?

1. A large difference between two companies:
After entering the market, the second company became ___ (successful).
2. Clear number one in a group:
It took ___ share of the market. (big)
3. Two changes developing together:
___ (intense / rivalry / become), ___ (mistakes / both companies / make).
Explain the difference: Give one true example with far more and one with by far. Your partner identifies whether you are comparing two things or ranking one item in a group.
