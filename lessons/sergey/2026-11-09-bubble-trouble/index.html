Lesson 03 / 09

From correct answers to natural sentences

8 minutes · repair your own English

Rewrite new situations naturally

All of devices in this category became unreliable after the update.
All + plural noun. Here the subject is plural, so use became (already correct).All the devices in this category became unreliable after the update.
Instead of the publisher fix the bug, it offered refunds.
Use instead of + -ing. Keeping the same subject in both clauses makes the sentence easier to follow.Instead of fixing the bug, the publisher offered refunds.
The internship offers plenty of possibilities to learn new skills.
An opportunity is a favourable situation that makes an action possible; a possibility is something that might happen. We usually say an opportunity to learn or an opportunity to do something.The internship offers plenty of opportunities to learn new skills.
There is very little chance the backup can be recovered.
Use chance of + -ing to talk about the likelihood of an action.There is very little chance of recovering the backup.
Style upgrade

Avoid repeating succeed / succeeding: “The city promises endless opportunities, but only a privileged few can actually get ahead.”

Lesson 09 / 09

The argument clinic

5–10 minutes · reserve discussion

1 · When does enthusiasm become hype?

Think of a game, device or service that attracted extraordinary attention. What evidence would separate a genuine breakthrough from a bubble?

Useful language · One sign of a real breakthrough is… · Hype becomes dangerous when… · I would look for…
Open a space to write (optional)
Show a possible answer

A breakthrough should produce measurable benefits. Hype becomes dangerous when people stop checking evidence and focus only on rising prices.

2 · What counts as a telling example?

A developer claims that one viral game proves the whole industry is thriving. Is that a representative example or a misleading one? Explain.

Useful language · It is not necessarily representative because… · A more telling indicator would be… · We should compare…
Open a space to write (optional)
Show a possible answer

One viral game is not necessarily representative. A more telling indicator would be how many studios remain profitable over several years.

3 · Should investors be protected?

A speculative game-related token collapses. Should regulators intervene, or should buyers accept the risk? Give one argument on each side.

Useful language · On the one hand… · On the other hand… · I would draw the line at…
Open a space to write (optional)
Show a possible answer

Buyers should understand the risk, but regulators should act when companies hide material information or make misleading promises.
