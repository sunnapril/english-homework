B2 speaking

Replace it now, repair it or wait?

Situation: Pavel's six-year-old television still works, but a dark line has appeared along one edge of the screen. A repair would cost €110 and include a six-month guarantee. A sturdy new model costs €620, or €56 a month for twelve months. Pavel is moving in eight months and does not yet know how large his next living room will be.

Choose a course of action. Take at least four details into account and use three expressions from the lesson.

Useful language: On the one hand, ... / appeal to / settle for / not set in stone / buy in instalments / take ... into account / recommend + -ing

Show a possible answer after Sergey has decided

I would recommend repairing the television for now. On the one hand, paying in instalments might make the new model more affordable; on the other hand, Pavel does not know what size will suit his next home. The move is fixed, but his future living arrangements are not set in stone. Taking the repair guarantee and the television's age into account, a temporary repair seems more reasonable than settling for an expensive model that may not fit the new room.
