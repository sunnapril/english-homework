4 · Grammar map · Language Bank 3B

Seven useful comparison patterns

1 · Difference
much / far / a lot more competitive
a bit / slightly less confident
2 · Similarity
as thoughtful as
not nearly as tough as
3 · Extreme
by far the toughest
easily the most remarkable
4 · Two people
Of the two, she is the more experienced.
5 · Proportion
The more confident he became, the better he played.
6 · Type / quality
He is more of a dreamer than a doer.
7 · Concession
As talented as he is, he still needs practice.
Meaning matters: much/far/a lot signal a big difference; a bit/slightly signal a small one. Nearly/almost means the difference is small; not nearly means it is large.
