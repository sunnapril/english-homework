02 · Word order

Put the marker in its natural place

Write each complete sentence. Include the question mark where you see ?.

Hint
Usually normally goes before an action verb.
2. to school / often / me / my mum / drives / .
Hint
Subject → frequency marker → action verb.
3. at home / usually / we / stay / don't / .
Hint
In a negative sentence, put usually after don’t.
4. with her mum / sometimes / cook / she / does / ?
Hint
Does → subject → frequency marker → base verb.

Hint

07 · Read and rebuild

A dessert they made themselves

Yesterday a girl and her mum decided to make tiramisu. First, they checked the recipe and prepared everything they needed. The girl mixed the ingredients, but she didn’t drink anything and soon felt thirsty. They put the dessert in the fridge and tried it later. They didn’t buy the dessert — they made it themselves. It was cold and creamy, and they were proud of the result.
1. What did they decide to make?
Hint
Answer with a complete sentence: They decided…
2. Did they buy the dessert?
Hint
Give a short negative answer.
3. Why did the girl feel thirsty?
Hint
Find the reason before the words felt thirsty.
4. When did they try the tiramisu?
Hint
The time word is in the fourth sentence.
5. Were they proud of the result?
Hint
The question starts with were, so the short answer does too.
