03 · Sentence map

How all three tenses work

TenseStatementNegativeQuestion
Present SimpleShe usually wears jeans.She doesn’t wear jeans.Does she wear jeans?
Present ContinuousShe is wearing jeans now.She isn’t wearing jeans.Is she wearing jeans?
Past Simple · actionShe wore jeans yesterday.She didn’t wear jeans.Did she wear jeans?
Past Simple · beShe was tired.She wasn’t tired.Was she tired?

05 · Word order

Build the complete sentence

The time marker is part of the sentence. Do not forget punctuation.

1. during breaks / checks / usually / her phone / she / .
Hint
Put usually before the action verb.
2. her favourite outfit / today / wearing / she / is / ?
Hint
Present Continuous question: Is → subject → verb-ing.
3. yesterday / the dessert / buy / didn't / they / .
Hint
After didn’t, use the base form.
4. two days ago / do / what / she / did / ?
Hint
Question word → did → subject → base verb.
5. after the dance class / thirsty / was / she / .
Hint
Thirsty is an adjective, so use be.
06 · Stop the automatic is

Keep the idea, repair the grammar

1. Is wear jeans usually.

Answer
She usually wears jeans.

2. Is made tiramisu yesterday.

Answer
She made tiramisu yesterday.

3. Did she was thirsty after dancing?

Answer
Was she thirsty after dancing?

4. She didn't decided to buy it.

Answer
She didn’t decide to buy it.
