01 · 5 min

Remember?

No word bank. The first letter is in the gap.

1I ___ (a…) with you: reading every day is a good idea.
Same opinion as another person.
agree
2Mum was ___ (a…) when she saw the mess in the kitchen.
A feeling. She is not happy with you!
angry (also: annoyed)
3Can I ___ (b…) your pen? I'll give it back after the lesson.
Take something and give it back later.
borrow
4We need to ___ (r…) the top of the hill before it gets dark.
Get to a place, often after a long way. One word.
reach
5The dog's ___ (o…) was looking for it everywhere.
The person the dog belongs to. A noun.
owner
6You look tired. You ___ (s…) go to bed earlier.
Give advice. The next verb is in its base form.
should
02 · 12 min

Word building

-mentverb → noun: enjoy → enjoyment, pay → payment
-fulnoun → adjective: colour → colourful, peace → peaceful
un-the opposite: unkind, unlucky, untidy

Change the word on the right so that it fits the sentence.

1There is a big ___ in your English this year. IMPROVE
After a big you need a noun.
improvement
2The school concert was very ___. Everybody loved it. SUCCESS
After was very you need an adjective.
successful
3Be ___ with the hot tea! CARE
An adjective. Think: be … !
careful
4This app is really ___: it shows when the next bus comes. USE
An adjective with a suffix.
useful
5My neighbour is very ___: she always fixes my bike for free. HELP
An adjective. She helps a lot.
helpful
6I felt ___ because I lost my favourite hoodie. HAPPY
The opposite meaning. Add a prefix.
unhappy
7It's ___ for my brother to get up early: he usually sleeps till ten. USUAL
The opposite meaning.
unusual
8The task was ___, so I asked the teacher to explain it again. CLEAR
She didn't understand it. The opposite meaning.
unclear
9Our teachers and students came to an ___ about the new school rules. AGREE
After an you need a noun.
agreement
03 · 15 min

in · on · at

inmonths, seasons, years, parts of the day
in May · in summer · in 2027 · in the morning
ondays and dates
on Friday · on Friday evening · on 5 March
atan exact time
at 7 o'clock · at lunchtime · at night (!)

Big → small: a long period → one day → one moment.

Write in, on or at.
