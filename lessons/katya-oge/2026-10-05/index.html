3 · Find the mistake

About 7 min · edit the sentence itself, then explain the rule
Routine + usually → Present Simple.
I usually go swimming after school.
Finished time marker: last year.
Last year I started modelling.
Present Perfect = have/has + past participle.
I haven't seen this film yet.
3rd person singular: does / doesn’t.
My friend doesn't enjoy competitions.
After did/didn’t, use the base form.
Yesterday we were very tired, so we didn’t go out.
will + base verb, no to.
I think I will study English next year.

7 · OGE speaking: answer + detail

About 10 min · first answer naturally, then improve

Imagine an electronic assistant is asking you questions.

1. How many days a week do you usually have classes?
2. What do you normally do after school?
3. How much time do you spend on homework?
4. What school subject do you find most difficult? Why?
5. Do you prefer studying alone or with someone? Why?
6. What would you recommend to a student who wants to improve their English?

Upgrade your answer

answer + detail / reason / example

Short: Three times a week.
Better: I train three times a week, usually in the evening after school.
