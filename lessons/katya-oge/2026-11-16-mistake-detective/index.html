Найди ошибку

В каждом предложении одна ошибка. Нажми на неё, потом напиши, как правильно (только исправленную часть).

1You should don't eat so much sugar before bed.
После should сразу идёт глагол. Отрицание — в самом should.
You shouldn't eat so much sugar before bed.
2My brother watched this series four times so far.
so far = до сих пор, к этому моменту. Какое время?
My brother has watched this series four times so far.
3By both of my friends love horror films.
Двое друзей, оба. Слово начинается на B — и всё.
both of my friends love horror films.
4The vet said my cat would get better, so I felt hopeless.
Новость хорошая. Полный надежды или без надежды?
The vet said my cat would get better, so I felt hopeful
5It was a difficult decidement, but I chose the cheaper trip.
Существительное от decide. Буква d меняется.
It was a difficult decision but I chose the cheaper trip.
6He wrote me a long apologise for breaking my headphones.
Нужно существительное: извинение.
He wrote me a long apology for breaking my headphones.
7We have visited the science museum last Friday.
last Friday — время закончилось.
We visited the science museum last Friday.
8Have you ever lend your bike to a friend?
Have you ever + третья форма. lend – lent – lent.
Have you ever lent your bike to a friend?

Детектив-аудирование

Это пересказ аудио, которое ты уже слушала в ДЗ. В пересказе 2 ошибки. Сначала прочитай, потом слушай и нажми на предложения, где ошибка. Потом скажи вслух, как правильно.

A · Четыре влога

3 мин
  1. Sam makes fashion videos and buys clothes with the money from his vlog.
  2. He makes only one video a week because he is busy with school.
  3. Lukas and Hanna play video games together in their daily vlog.
  4. On Saturdays they do their homework.✓ On Saturdays they edit their videos. They do homework on Sundays.
  5. Demi talks about beauty products, and two million people watch her videos.
  6. She can relax on the bus because nobody recognises her there.✓ She can never relax on the bus: everybody takes photos of her.
  7. Megan, Tom’s cousin, plays the guitar in her vlog and gives lessons online.
  8. Tom hasn’t got a vlog because some people write horrible comments.
Текст аудио (открыть, если нужно)

Sam: Hi guys. I’m Sam. I buy clothes with the money from my vlog. Then I get dressed in the clothes and I talk about fashion in my videos. I only make one video a week because I’m busy with school. Like most people in Mumbai, I get up early. My family and I have breakfast, and Mum and Dad go to work at 11 a.m., but I have lessons at half past eight. After school, I go home and I can’t relax because I do my homework for three hours.

Lukas: Hi there, I’m Lukas, I’m sixteen and this is my sister, Hanna.

Hanna: Hi, I’m sixteen too. Lukas and I have got a daily vlog. We play video games together. It’s fun!

Lukas: Yes, but I’m tired, and I want to spend more time with my friends. My best friend finishes school, goes home, listens to music or goes out, but I can’t see him because we make videos every day.

Hanna: That’s true. Our weekends are busy too. On Saturdays we edit videos, and on Sundays we do our homework.

Demi: Hey, I’m Demi. Lots of people want to become vloggers. They think it’s easy. In my videos, I put on make-up, I brush my teeth and hair and I talk about beauty products. I do this nearly every day because 2 million people watch my videos! I’ve got lots of money, and that’s good, but the problem is that I work all the time. I never relax when I take the bus or have dinner in my home town because everybody takes photos of me. I like vlogging, but I want some free time too.

Tom: Hi. I’m Tom, and my cousin, Megan, lives in Cape Town. Megan plays the guitar in her vlog and people have guitar lessons with her online. She gets up late, has lunch, has a shower, gets dressed in jeans and a T-shirt and then she makes videos all afternoon. She loves vlogging, but some people write horrible comments. For example, they say ‘You can’t play the guitar well’ or ‘I hate your music’. This is why I haven’t got a vlog.

1 мин

  4. In the evening she does yoga for twenty minutes.✓ In the evening she does yoga for 30 minutes.

Текст аудио (открыть, если нужно)

Molly: Yoga is part of my daily routine. I do it for ten minutes in my bedroom when I get up in the morning. Then I have a shower, get dressed, have some breakfast and go to school. And I’m ready to study!

Molly: After school, I have lots of things to do: piano lessons on Tuesdays and tennis lessons on Thursdays. Then, in the evening, I have dinner and do my homework in the dining room. There’s no time to watch TV!

Molly: I’m always tired after my busy day, but I can’t relax. That’s when I do more yoga. I do it for 30 minutes before I go to bed, and then it’s easy to sleep!

Molly: Now, it’s time for you to do some yoga … You’re probably new to yoga, but don’t worry about it … just watch, listen and copy me …
