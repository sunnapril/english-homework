# Homework & lesson techniques (Irina's standard, 12.11.2026)

Applies to every student, every homework (site page or Google Doc) and every lesson page.

## Always (hard rules)
1. Word bank is **shuffled** (never in question order) and has **one extra item** ("One word is extra").
2. On lesson pages the word bank is **sticky** (stays under the tabs while scrolling).
3. No source / credit lines ("Adapted from Roadmap…"), no "REVIEW" or teacher notes on student pages.
4. Situations are natural, specific (place, time, reason, all needed details) and **not repeated** from earlier lessons; the character is not a "mirror" of the student; rule examples do not repeat the exercise sentences.
5. Hints guide but never contain the answer or its form; a word in brackets ≠ the answer.
6. New / target words are **bold and coloured** (rules, Useful language, texts, word banks in Docs).
7. Contexts come from the student's interests (Sergey: games, IT; Natalia: travel, theatre, art; Katya: OGE topics).
8. Workbook (WB) only in homework, never in a lesson.
9. Every gap has one answer or all correct answers are listed; one Check per block, "Show correct answer" + "Show all correct answers".
10. Speaking: Ideas → Useful beginnings → "✓ I said my answer" → Example answer → "Your turn — change at least two things".

## Role-model techniques (from @a_zephyrus_)
- **Summary line at the top** ("Итог"): what to submit and the deadline, in one line.
- **Time per block** in the heading (e.g. `2 · LISTENING · 10 min`).
- **Mini glossary before audio/video**: 4–6 words with short meanings, so the listening is not blocked by vocabulary.
- **Detective listening**: a short text/podcast retelling with **2 planted factual mistakes**; the student finds and corrects them.
- **Personal part**: one task tied to the student's own interest (a game, a trip, a show).
- **Russian instructions for A1–A2** students (English for B1+).
- **Exit ticket**: 2–3 quick questions at the end ("one new phrase I'll use", "one thing that was hard").
- **Shout-out** for completed homework at the start of the next lesson (one specific good sentence from the student).
