# Lesson scenarios (reusable structures)

## Behind the Door · mystery lesson (idea for Sergey, 31.10.2026)
Use with the **current unit's topic and grammar**, not an old topic. 60 minutes.
1. **Set-up (5 min):** one picture + 5-line story with a mystery. Question: *What might have happened?*
2. **Doors (30 min):** 6 doors, each hides a short clue (5–8 lines, the student's level): a note, a page of a diary or manuscript, a news item, a report, a message. After each door the student draws a conclusion with the unit's target grammar. One or two false clues train logic.
3. **Solution (10 min):** the student builds a version (who, what, why), then compares it with the "official" card.
4. **Vocabulary (10 min):** 8–10 words from the clues → later added to the student's word quiz.
5. **Homework:** a detective's report (120–150 words for B2) using the target grammar.
Atmosphere: noir / paranormal, never gory (Sergey likes horror stories but doesn't like being scared).
